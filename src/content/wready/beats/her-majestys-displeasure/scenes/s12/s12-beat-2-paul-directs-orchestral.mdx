s-INT. EMI STUDIOS, STUDIO ONE - DAY:
a-The vast, hallowed space, normally home to rock and roll, is now filled with a 30-PIECE ORCHESTRA. String, woodwind, and brass sections, all in formal attire. The air is one of hushed, professional reverence.

a-Paul stands beside the CONDUCTOR (60s, distinguished, a George Martin type), looking slightly out of his element but exuding a quiet confidence. He's listening intently, the professional.

c-PAUL
p-(quietly, to the Conductor)
d-It needs to feel hopeful, but with a bit of a northern sadness to it. You know? Like a Sunday afternoon when the rain's just stopped.

a-The Conductor nods, understanding completely. He raises his baton.

c-CONDUCTOR
d-From the top, gentlemen. "Love in the Open Air."

a-The baton falls. The orchestra begins to play Paul's theme. It's lush, romantic, and sophisticated. It sounds like a 'proper' piece of film music. Paul closes his eyes, listening, a small smile playing on his lips. This is different. This is legitimacy.

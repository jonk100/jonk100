# The Target's Evolution: From Beatle to Dissident

John Lennon's journey from pop phenomenon to political symbol, driven by intellectual restlessness and the need for authentic expression—even when it becomes institutional ammunition.

## Key Elements

### Early Phase: Spiritual and Intellectual Questioning
- Reads widely, questions celebrity, seeks meaning beyond pop fame
- The Cleave interview becomes the "Jesus" controversy that launches the covert operation
- Retreat from touring into studio experimentation and artistic radicalization

### Middle Phase: Personal and Political Transformation
- Yoko Ono arrives as mirror and collaborator, not disruptor
- Domestic unraveling with Cynthia parallels artistic deconstruction
- Apple Corps represents naive bid for control that becomes another cage

### Late Phase: Political Symbolism
- Returns MBE as explicit political act (Vietnam/Biafra + chart joke)
- Peace activism and bed-ins turn personal evolution into public theater
- Becomes the specific target of assassination while remaining largely unaware of the conspiracy

## Irony and Awareness

- **Unknowing target:** John never learns about the full scale of the operation against him
- **Institutional blindness:** The Crown mistakes organic artistic evolution for conspiracy-worthy threat
- **Personal cost:** John's authenticity destroys his first marriage while making him politically significant

## Relationship to Other Plots

- **Target of:** The Crown's Covert War (A-plot) and Operatives' Divergence (B-plot)
- **Drives:** Beatles Creative Fracture (E-subplot) through his artistic evolution
- **Parallel to:** Queen Elizabeth's wisdom (D-plot) in understanding institutional limits

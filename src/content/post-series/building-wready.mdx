wReady is a personal creative workbench — a single-developer tool for managing screenplays, songs, poems, and interconnected creative content. This series walks through how it's built, why certain architectural decisions were made, and how you can apply similar patterns to your own projects.

Each post is both a tutorial and a design document. We'll cover Astro 6's content collections system, building a custom MDX plugin, vanilla CSS patterns for editorial interfaces, and how to structure TypeScript utilities for creative applications.

Whether you're building a creative tool, a portfolio site, or a content management system for yourself, you'll find patterns here worth stealing.

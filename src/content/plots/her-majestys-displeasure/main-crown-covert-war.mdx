# Operation Minuet → Operation Elegy

The Crown's covert campaign against the Beatles begins with Prince Philip's personal outrage at the MBE ceremony and the "more popular than Jesus" controversy. What starts as psychological manipulation (Operation Minuet) escalates to assassination (Operation Elegy).

## Key Elements

- **Inciting incident:** Philip perceives the Beatles' cultural authority as a threat to institutional dignity
- **Chain of command:** Philip → Mountbatten → Finchley → Nigel & Clive
- **Tactics evolution:** From manufactured outrage to direct elimination
- **Institutional escalation:** Each perceived success justifies more extreme measures
- **Ultimate failure:** The conspiracy collapses while the Beatles' dissolution was already inevitable

## Relationship to Other Plots

- **Parent to:** Klaus Infiltration (F-subplot), Press as Weapon (G-subplot)
- **Drives:** Nigel & Clive's moral divergence (B-plot)
- **Opposes:** John Lennon's radicalization (C-plot) and Queen Elizabeth's passive resistance (D-plot)

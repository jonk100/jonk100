The `Callout` component is a block-level alert designed for long-form reading content (like MDX articles and documentation). Unlike the `Alert` component (which is more UI-focused), `Callout` is optimized for integrating within prose to highlight tips, warnings, and contextual notes.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Contextual Warnings and Tips
Using callouts to deliver important information without breaking the reader's flow:

<ComponentPreview title="Documentation Callouts">
  <div style="width: 100%; text-align: left;">
    <Stack gap="md">
      <Callout theme="primary" title="Pro Tip">
        You can use the <code>Ctrl + K</code> shortcut to quickly open the command palette anywhere in the application.
      </Callout>
      <Callout theme="warning" title="Deprecation Notice">
        The v1 API endpoints will be officially retired on August 1st. Please migrate to v2 as soon as possible.
      </Callout>
      <Callout theme="danger" title="Critical Failure">
        Do not share your master database password in public repositories.
      </Callout>
    </Stack>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

The `Paper` component is our foundational surface block, extending the standard HTML `<div>` element. When building a design system, interfaces require clear visual hierarchy. We standardized `Paper` to consolidate surface elevation variables, shadow calculations, and container paddings into a single, cohesive modular wrapper.

### Elevations & Interactive Press
To create visual depth and physical tactility, `Paper` includes two core interaction systems:
1. **The Science of Elevations**: It maps layers `"-1"` through `"5"` to progressive HSL custom properties. Elevated layers progressively mix lighter values and scale box shadows to create a realistic visual illusion of components floating closer to the light source.
2. **Interactive Tactility**: Setting `interactive={true}` applies high-performance GPU transitions. On hover, the surface "lifts" via a negative Y-translation and increased shadow. On press (`:active`), it subtly scales down to `0.98`, mimicking the physical resistance of a mechanical button.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Elevation Progression Grid
Instead of manually declaring individual background colors and shadow offsets across your stylesheet, you can simply adjust the `layer` parameter:

<ComponentPreview
  title="Progression of Elevation Surfaces"
>
  <Grid cols={3} gap="sm" style="width: 100%;">
    <Paper layer={-1} padding="sm" bordered={true}>
      <Text style="margin: 0; font-size: var(--text-xs); text-align: center;">Recessed (layer -1)</Text>
    </Paper>
    <Paper layer={1} padding="sm" bordered={true}>
      <Text style="margin: 0; font-size: var(--text-xs); text-align: center;">Elevated Layer 1</Text>
    </Paper>
    <Paper layer={2} padding="sm" bordered={true}>
      <Text style="margin: 0; font-size: var(--text-xs); text-align: center;">Elevated Layer 2</Text>
    </Paper>
  </Grid>
</ComponentPreview>

The `Alert` component provides a high-visibility container for critical system messages. Standardizing this primitive ensures that users can instantly recognize the semantic meaning of a message—such as a successful operation or a breaking system error—through consistent color coding and integrated iconography.

### Semantic Icon Mapping
To reduce boilerplate and ensure visual consistency, the component automatically maps its `theme` to a corresponding system icon. For example, setting `theme="success"` automatically injects the `check-circle` icon, while `theme="danger"` defaults to `alert-circle`. This guarantees that the visual language remains uniform across the entire application without requiring manual icon configuration for every callout.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Feedback Tones
Display the standard variety of semantic alerts to verify color contrast and icon alignment:

<ComponentPreview
  title="Semantic Alert Variations"
>
  <Stack gap="sm" style="width: 100%; max-width: 500px; margin: 0 auto;">
    <Alert theme="success" title="Deployment Successful">
      The production build has been successfully compiled and distributed.
    </Alert>
    <Alert theme="warning" title="Resource Limit Reached">
      Your account is approaching the monthly bandwidth limit.
    </Alert>
    <Alert theme="danger" title="Connection Failed">
      Unable to establish a secure handshake with the database cluster.
    </Alert>
  </Stack>
</ComponentPreview>

#### Advanced: Complex Body Content
Alerts can house complex nested content beyond simple strings, including multiple paragraphs or list structures, while maintaining rigid internal layouts:

    </Alert>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

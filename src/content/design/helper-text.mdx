The `HelperText` component is a low-level typographic primitive utilized by higher-order components (like `Label` and `Input`) to display status messages. When `variant="danger"` is supplied, the component automatically sets `role="alert"`, ensuring that assistive technologies announce the error immediately to the user.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Form Instructions and Alerts
Using `HelperText` manually to build custom form interactions outside the standard `Label` wrapper:

<ComponentPreview title="Contextual Input Hints">
  <div style="width: 100%; max-width: 400px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left;">
    <Stack gap="xs">
      <Input placeholder="Create a master password" type="password" />
      <HelperText variant="danger" size="xs">Password must be at least 12 characters long.</HelperText>
    </Stack>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

The `Quote` primitive provides a standardized, aesthetically pleasing wrapper for the native HTML `<blockquote>` element. It handles the complex layout requirements of combining italicized text with structured attribution data, ensuring consistency across articles and case studies.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Standard Blockquote
The default variant features a heavy left border and a subtle background tint, ideal for embedding quotes within standard prose:

<ComponentPreview title="Standard Blockquote">
  <div style="width: 100%; max-width: 500px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left;">
    <Quote 
      text="We are not now that strength which in old days moved earth and heaven; that which we are, we are."
      author="Alfred, Lord Tennyson"
      source="Ulysses, 1842"
    />
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

#### Advanced: Centered Pull-Quote
The `pull` variant is designed to break up long sections of text by aggressively centering a key phrase with top and bottom divider lines:

<ComponentPreview title="Centered Pull-Quote">
  <div style="width: 100%; max-width: 600px; margin: 0 auto;">
    <Quote 
      variant="pull"
      text="The medium is the message."
      author="Marshall McLuhan"
      source="Understanding Media, 1964"
    />
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

The `Divider` is our most minimalist structural primitive. It wraps the standard HTML `<hr>` element, providing a semantic and accessible way to signal a conceptual break in content without introducing the visual weight of a card or a container.

### Clean Border Implementation
To ensure that dividers remain crisp and consistent across all screen resolutions, the component utilizes a `1px` high border rendered in the theme's `currentColor` or a specific border token. By defaulting to the `hr` tag, it also provides the correct semantic signals to screen readers, indicating a thematic break in the document flow.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Content Separation
Use the divider to separate text blocks within an article or a list:

<ComponentPreview
  title="Thematic Content Break"
>
  <div style="width: 100%; max-width: 400px; margin: 0 auto; text-align: left;">
    <Stack gap="md">
      <Text type="body">The first phase of the operation involves initializing the local registry and validating all environmental secrets.</Text>
      <Divider />
      <Text type="body">Once validated, the system proceeds to establish a secure handshake with the remote database cluster.</Text>
    </Stack>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

#### Advanced: Integrated Layout Rules
Dividers are frequently utilized inside larger components (like `SectionLabel` or `Stack`) to create complex architectural lines. Here, we show a divider used to separate a header from its content within a constrained panel:

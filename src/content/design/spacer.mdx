The `Spacer` component provides a semantic way to introduce whitespace into layouts without relying on ad-hoc margins. By binding to the global spacing scale, it ensures vertical and horizontal rhythms remain consistent.

### Axis Awareness
The spacer can stretch vertically (`y`), horizontally (`x`), or in both directions. This makes it versatile for pushing content apart in flex containers or creating breathing room in vertical document flows.

### Usage Blueprints

#### Common: Vertical Rhythm
Using a spacer to separate two content blocks:

<ComponentPreview title="Vertical Spacer">
  <div style="width: 100%; border: 1px dashed var(--border-subtle); padding: var(--space-4);">
    <Box layer={1} padding="sm"><Text style="margin: 0; text-align: center;">Block A</Text></Box>
    <Spacer size="lg" axis="y" />
    <Box layer={1} padding="sm"><Text style="margin: 0; text-align: center;">Block B</Text></Box>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>

#### Advanced: Horizontal Flex Separation
Using a spacer within a flex container to push items apart:

<ComponentPreview title="Horizontal Spacer">
  <div style="width: 100%; display: flex; align-items: center; border: 1px dashed var(--border-subtle); padding: var(--space-4);">
    <Box layer={1} padding="sm"><Text style="margin: 0;">Left Item</Text></Box>
    <Spacer size="xl" axis="x" />
    <Box layer={1} padding="sm"><Text style="margin: 0;">Right Item</Text></Box>
  </div>
</ComponentPreview>
